Privacy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: Mangiamundo works without an account or any personal details. It keeps only what the game needs: which restaurants you checked in at, and, if you choose to sign in, your email address. No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is sold.

Who is responsible

Mangiamundo is run by Lightcone, Eeuwfeeststraat 51, 3010 Kessel-Lo, Belgium (company number BE1002.736.213). For any question about your data, or to have it deleted, write to hello@mangiamundo.com.

What we store, and why

  • Your account. On first use the app gets a random account, with no name or email. Each device signed in to it has a session: the device's name (such as "iPhone" or "Firefox on Mac") and when it was first and last used, so you can see and sign out your devices.
  • Check-ins. Which places you checked in at and on which day, and whether you were at the place when you did. That is the game: countries tasted, levels and badges.
  • Your location, only when you use it. To find the city you're in, measure distances to restaurants, or check that you're at a place when checking in, the app sends your position to our server. We use it for that request and don't store it. To find your city, the server asks OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service with that position.
  • Places you add or report as closed. We keep them with your account, and send the place's name, position and cuisine (or that it closed) to OpenStreetMap as a public note, without your name or account.
  • Reminders. If you turn on reminders on the website, we store your browser's push address, your area and your time zone, to send celebration reminders and news about new food nearby. The apps schedule reminders on your phone instead.
  • Signing in (optional). If you sign in with Apple or Google, we store the account id they give us and the email address they share (with Apple this can be a private relay address). If you sign in with a code by email, we store your email address; the code itself is kept only as a hash, for 10 minutes.
  • Recovery and link codes. Kept only as hashes, so we can't read them.
  • Sharing (optional). When you share your map or a check-in, we make a public page with it. Your map's page shows your area, level, countries per continent and badges; a shared check-in shows the place, its food and the date. You can turn your map's link off in Settings; removing a check-in removes its page.
  • On your device. The website keeps your session and settings (theme, chosen city, recent cities) in your browser's local storage. It sets no cookies.

We use this to provide the app you asked for (the GDPR's "performance of a contract"). Reminders, sign-in and sharing happen only when you turn them on, and you can turn them off again.

Google user data

Signing in with Google is optional. If you choose "Sign in with Google", Mangiamundo asks Google only for the basic sign-in scopes openid, email and profile. This is how Mangiamundo handles the Google user data it receives:

  • Data accessed: your Google account id, your email address and whether Google has verified it, and, as part of the profile scope, your name and profile picture. We request no other Google data: not your contacts, Gmail, Drive, calendar or anything else.
  • Data use: the account id and email address are used only to sign you in, so the same Mangiamundo account opens on every device where you sign in, and to show in Settings which Google account you're signed in with. Your name and profile picture are not used.
  • Data storage: we store only the Google account id and email address, with your Mangiamundo account, in our database at Fly.io in Amsterdam. Your name and profile picture are not stored.
  • Data sharing: we don't sell, rent or share Google user data with anyone, don't transfer it to third parties, don't use it for advertising, and don't use it to train AI models.
  • Data protection: all traffic to Mangiamundo is encrypted (HTTPS). The database is a file on our server's own disk, not reachable from the internet, and only we can access the hosting account.
  • Data retention and deletion: we keep it as long as you have a Mangiamundo account. It is deleted, together with everything else, when you delete your account (Settings → Delete my account), or within 30 days of a request to hello@mangiamundo.com. You can also revoke Mangiamundo's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Mangiamundo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Signing in with Apple

If you choose "Sign in with Apple", we receive your Apple account id and the email address you choose to share (which can be a private relay address). We use and store them in the same way as Google's above: only to sign you in, never shared, and deleted with your account. You can stop using Sign in with Apple with Mangiamundo in your Apple ID settings.

Who else handles it

We use a few services to run Mangiamundo. They process data only to provide their service to us.

  • Fly.io hosts our server and database, in Amsterdam. Like any server, it keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service running.
  • Cloudflare handles the mangiamundo.com domain and passes traffic to our server.
  • Resend sends sign-in codes to your email address.
  • Apple and Google, when you choose to sign in with them, under their own privacy policies.
  • OpenStreetMap provides the restaurants and areas, and receives the notes about added and closed places described above.
  • Push services of your browser (Apple, Google or Mozilla) deliver web reminders.

Some of these companies are based outside the European Union; where data leaves the EU, it is covered by the safeguards the GDPR requires, such as the EU's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

As long as you have an account. You can delete it at any time in the app under Settings → Delete my account: everything above goes at once, on all your devices. Places you added stay on OpenStreetMap if its mappers accepted them, without any link to you.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, write to hello@mangiamundo.com. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (dataprotectionauthority.be).

Changes

If we change how we use your data, we'll update this page and its date, and mention bigger changes in the app.
